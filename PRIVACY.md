# Privacy Policy

PrivaBlock collects no data and has no servers.

- **Stored in your browser only:** your settings, your filters, the list of sites where you turned
  PrivaBlock off, blocking statistics, and downloaded filter lists. Uninstalling PrivaBlock removes them.
- **Logger:** records a tab's requests only while the logger page is open, in memory, and forgets them
  when it closes.
- **Network requests:** PrivaBlock downloads the filter lists you enabled directly from their publishers
  (on Chromium, only the custom lists you added). Those publishers can see your IP address like any web
  server; their own privacy policies apply. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
- **Fingerprinting protection (Firefox, optional):** changes Firefox's own setting while turned on and
  gives it back when turned off.
